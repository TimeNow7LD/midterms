Why squares instead of a map?

Every state has exactly one governor, so every state gets the same size square. On a regular map, big, empty states like Montana dominate the picture, while small ones like Rhode Island nearly disappear, even though each counts the same.

How to read it

  • Solid colors are races decided in 2026.
  • Pale colors are the 14 states that didn't elect a governor this year. Kentucky, Louisiana and Mississippi vote in 2027. New Jersey and Virginia vote in 2029. The rest vote in 2028.
  • Striped squares are races not yet confirmed in the sources we use. They're filled in as results are called.
  • A white dot marks a state that flipped parties in 2026: Texas, Georgia, Ohio, Iowa and Nevada.

When do they take office?

Most new governors are sworn in during January 2027, but the exact date varies by state. The new Congress is sworn in on January 3, 2027.

Sources and method

  • Race calls: NBC News projections, with Florida from The Washington Post and Vermont from WCAX. A state is shown as decided only when a winner has been projected by a news organization or the opponent has conceded.
  • Races are not final until each state certifies its results.
  • Party shown is the governor's party. Minnesota's Democratic-Farmer-Labor Party is shown as Democratic.
